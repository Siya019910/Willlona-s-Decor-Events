Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 months in advance for large events like weddings, and 4 weeks for smaller celebrations to ensure availability.
Do you offer custom decor packages?
Yes! Every event is unique. We tailor our packages to suit your specific theme, budget, and guest count.
Do you handle setup and breakdown?
Absolutely. Our team manages the full installation before the event and the cleanup afterward, so you can focus on enjoying your moment.
